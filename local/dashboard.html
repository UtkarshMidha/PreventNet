How To Read This Chart
              
    
                
  • Blue, train accuracy: share of the 615 training people classified correctly.
    • 
                
  • Orange, test accuracy: the same on 153 people the model never saw.
    • 
                
  • Green, test AUC: chance that someone who developed diabetes is ranked above someone who didn't (0.5 = coin flip, 1.0 = perfect).
    • 
                
  • Flat lines mean the model has finished learning.

The risk model learns from the public Pima Indians Diabetes dataset: 768 women aged 21 and over, 268 (34.9%) of whom developed diabetes within five years. Its columns are split across the three sources. The prescription column is simulated. Figures are totals each source computes itself.
